About Lisa

Personal trainer, nutritionist, and a firm believer in balance.

Hi! I'm Lisa Brown.

I have been educating people about fitness and nutrition professionally since I was 16 years old. At 52, I bring 40 years of hands-on experience in training and nutrition to every client I work with.

Fitness should be a part of your life and not rule it.

I practice what I preach. My approach is about balance: sustainable habits you can keep for life, not quick fixes that fade in a few weeks.

My goal is to give you an education and teach you how to eat and train for your goals. Honestly, I want to coach myself out of a job, because when you understand the why, you don't need me forever.
